Simple steps to get started

See how easy it is to transfer a design onto a tea towel

No drawing skills needed. Print your design, then use fabric transfer paper to trace the outlines onto your tea towel before painting.

01

Print your design

Choose one of the 120 designs and print it on US Letter or A4 paper. Select Fit to Page if needed.

02 TRANSFER PAPER

Place the transfer paper

Place the fabric transfer paper on the towel with the transfer side facing the fabric.

03

Add your printed design

Lay the printed design over the transfer paper and secure the edges so it stays in place.

04

Trace the outlines

Trace the design with a pencil or stylus to transfer the outlines onto the towel.

05

Remove the paper and paint

Lift off the design and transfer paper. The outlines will be on the fabric, ready to paint.
